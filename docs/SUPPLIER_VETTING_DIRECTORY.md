## 1. Directory Overview & Factory Cluster Strategy

CurateMark operates an asset-light, zero-inventory supply chain by directly interfacing with verified Indian manufacturing clusters. Every supplier in this directory has been vetted against five non-negotiable criteria:
1. **Zero Intellectual Property Infringement:** 100% original, unbranded blanks or custom private-label production. Zero counterfeit / 7A replicas.
2. **Blind-Dropship Capability:** Ability to ship parcels directly to the end consumer with CurateMark's custom shipping label, branded packaging, and packing slip — with zero supplier branding on the box or tape.
3. **API / Webhook Automation:** Programmatic order ingestion, inventory synchronization, and automated AWB / tracking number return.
4. **Dispatch SLA:** Maximum 24 to 48 hours order processing time for ready blanks and compounding batches.
5. **Quality Assurance Benchmark:** Minimum 99.2% defect-free dispatch rate, backed by contractual defect penalty credits.

```
[ Step 1: NDA & Commercial Agreement ]

                  │

```

### Step 1: Formal Agreement & NDA
Execute the CurateMark Standard OEM Vendor Agreement containing:
* Strict Non-Circumvention / Non-Disclosure: Supplier cannot contact customers or use order data.
* Defect Penalty Clause: 100% invoice credit + ₹150 courier penalty for any product dispatched with manufacturing defects.
* Packaging Exclusivity: Supplier agrees never to insert factory marketing materials, business cards, or generic manufacturer invoices.

### Step 2: Sample Inspection & Laboratory Testing
Before greenlighting any SKU:
* Apparel: Cut test, wash test at 40°C, shrinkage measurement (must be <3%), seam tension test.
* Fragrances: 8-hour skin blotter evaluation, gas chromatography certificate verification for IFRA purity, atomizer mist dispersion test.
* Jewelry: 72-hour saline immersion test, scratch resistance test.
* Footwear: Flex test (50,000 cycles on sole bonding machine), leather grain cross-section inspection.

### Step 3: Branded Packaging Consignment
CurateMark prints bulk packaging via regional packaging partners (Upack / DCGPAC) and consigns inventory to the supplier's dispatch bay:
* 1,000 Custom Poly Mailers sent to Printrove & Qikink.
* 500 Custom Microfiber Pouches sent to Rajkot Jewelry Lab.
* 250 Rigid Magnetic Shoeboxes sent to Agra Leather Workshop.
* 250 Rigid Fragrance Boxes sent to Kannauj Compounding Facility.

### Step 4: Technical Integration (Shiprocket / Custom Webhook)
Suppliers are connected to CurateMark's multi-vendor dispatch engine:
* When an order is placed on CurateMark.in, a Shiprocket/Delhivery shipping label (PDF) and Packing Slip are generated via API.
* The supplier's dispatch station receives an automated order notification with the label attached.
* The supplier prints the label, packs the item in CurateMark packaging, slaps the label on the box, and hands it to the assigned courier pickup agent.

## 4. Defect Management & Return Handling SOP

To preserve CurateMark's lean operating model:
1. **Open-Box Unboxing Video Policy:** Customers are instructed on the packaging tape and order receipt to record a 30-second unboxing video.
2. **Defective Item Replacement:**
   * If a customer submits a valid video showing damage or incorrect sizing, CurateMark initiates an immediate reverse-pickup via Delhivery and triggers a replacement order to the OEM.
   * The defective item is routed back to the OEM's warehouse.
   * OEM absorbs the replacement COGS and reverse freight charges under the vendor SLA.
3. **Return Thresholds:** Any OEM SKU that exceeds a 2.5% defect rate over a 30-day window is instantly paused for factory re-audit.
