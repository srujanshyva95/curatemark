## 5. Working Capital & Cash Flow Management

### 5.1 Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)
Because CurateMark operates without warehousing holding costs:
* **Prepaid UPI Orders (55%–65% of volume):** Payment settles into CurateMark's bank account in T+1 days via Razorpay/PhonePe. Factory is paid weekly net-7. **Cash conversion cycle is negative (-6 days)** — CurateMark holds the customer's cash before paying the OEM.
* **Verified COD Orders (35%–45% of volume):** Customer pays ₹200 advance UPI immediately (T+1 settlement). Courier collects balance at doorstep and remits via COD Remittance cycle in T+4 to T+7 days.
