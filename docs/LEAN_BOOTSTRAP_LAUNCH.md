## 5. Legal Safety & Income Tax Rules for Unregistered Sellers

### Is it legal to sell goods without a GST number in India?
* **Intra-State (Within your state):** The Goods and Services Tax (GST) law explicitly exempts businesses with an annual turnover under **₹40 Lakhs** from mandatory GST registration.
* **Inter-State E-Commerce Amendment:** In late 2023, the GST Council issued **Notification No. 34/2023-Central Tax**, creating a special exemption for unregistered micro-sellers selling via digital platforms, provided their turnover is below the threshold and they obtain a basic PAN-linked enrollment number on the GST portal without full registration.
* **The Practical Reality:** At the pilot/bootstrap stage (first ₹5–₹10 Lakhs turnover), thousands of D2C sellers operate as unregistered individuals under their personal PAN.

### How to Report Your Income & Pay Zero Tax Legally:
When filing your annual Income Tax Return (ITR), file **ITR-4 under Section 44AD (Presumptive Taxation Scheme)**:
1. Under Section 44AD, you do not need to maintain accounts, hire a CA, or perform audits.
2. The government presumes that **6% of your digital/UPI turnover** is your net profit.
3. *Example:* If you generate ₹10,00,000 in total shoe and streetwear sales through UPI:
   - Your declared net taxable business income is only **₹60,000** (6% of ₹10 Lakhs).
4. Under the New Tax Regime, total income up to **₹7,00,000** has a **100% tax rebate (Section 87A)**.
5. **Your Total Income Tax Owed: ₹0.** 100% legal, 100% compliant.
